## Claim judgment

- **C1 (fixed controls reproduce P0): yes for the arithmetic/control-matching part.** Every original linear-QRF and rolling-CQR macro coverage/width/IS/tail-miss value matches P0; forest continuity itself is outside this subreview. This establishes reproduction, not superiority or independent confirmation.
- **C2 (a candidate achieves the joint ramp/near-zero coverage, width and score goals): no.** No eligible candidate passes all nine criteria on development validation or either evaluation. The report's negative conclusion at `REPORT.md:7`–`:11` is supported. Higher coverage alone is insufficient.
- **C3 (descriptive gains/tradeoffs): yes for the explicitly narrow statements; partial for any mechanistic/general claim.** In 2014 conditional DualTail raises ramp/near-zero coverage **1.533356/3.607920 percentage points**, but width grows **2.770025%**, with overall IS/WIS worse. In 2012 conditional PIT improves WIS **3.141396%** against CQR and CRPS **5.02%** against ECDF, but near-zero coverage drops **3.041195 percentage points**. These do not establish the effects of state conditioning, boundary extension, tie randomization, a physical zero-mass mechanism, future-deployment validity, or broad superiority. Saved non-nesting counts are accurately reported; the full-law arms have zero violations, whereas interval-only arms do not automatically define a consistent joint quantile law.
